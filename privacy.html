Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 11, 2026 • Effective immediately

1. Overview

OpenFishTools Studio ("we", "our", or "the app") is a high-performance web-based motion graphics, animation, and video editing tool developed by Cutefish. We are deeply committed to protecting your personal privacy. OpenFishTools Studio operates with a client-first, privacy-respecting architecture.

2. Data Collection & Local Processing

OpenFishTools Studio processes your animations, layers, keyframes, effects, and media files entirely within your web browser using client-side technologies (HTML5 Canvas, Web Audio API, and IndexedDB). We do not operate external database servers that upload, catalog, or inspect your creative projects.

3. Google OAuth 2.0 & Google Drive Integration

OpenFishTools Studio offers an optional Cloud Project Sync feature allowing you to save and synchronize your projects across your personal devices via Google Drive.

When you choose to connect your Google Account:

  • Requested Scope: We request the restricted scope https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file. This scope only permits OpenFishTools Studio to create and access files that the app itself has created (projects stored in your OpenFishTools Projects folder with the .ofts extension). The app cannot view, edit, read, or delete any other files or folders in your Google Drive.
  • Profile Information: We only access your basic account display name, email address, and profile picture to identify your active session within the app interface.
  • Storage Location: All project data is stored directly in your own personal Google Drive account. We do not transmit or duplicate your Google Drive files to any third-party servers.

Google API Services User Data Policy Compliance:
OpenFishTools Studio's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

4. Local Storage & Session Data

The app uses browser storage (localStorage and sessionStorage) strictly for application functionality, such as:

  • Preserving temporary OAuth authentication tokens during your active session.
  • Remembering your active editor settings and theme preferences.
  • Caching local project states for offline resilience.

This data remains on your physical device and is never transmitted to any marketing or analytics platforms.

5. Information Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, trade, or otherwise share your personal information, project files, Google Drive contents, or user data with third parties under any circumstances.

6. Data Deletion and Revocation

You have full control over your data at all times:

  • Disconnecting Google Drive: You can sign out at any time via the Sign Out button in the Cloud Projects tab.
  • Revoking Access: You can permanently revoke OpenFishTools Studio's access to your Google Account at any time via Google Account Permissions.
  • Deleting Projects: You can delete any project directly inside OpenFishTools Studio or by opening Google Drive and deleting the project file or the OpenFishTools Projects folder.
