Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 11, 2026 • Effective immediately

1. Overview

FishTools Studio ("we", "our", or "the app") is a web-based motion graphics, animation, and video editing tool developed by Cutefish. We are committed to protecting your privacy. FishTools Studio operates with a client-first, privacy-respecting architecture.

2. Data Collection & Local Processing

FishTools Studio processes your animations, layers, keyframes, effects, and media files entirely within your web browser using client-side technologies (HTML5 Canvas, Web Audio API, and IndexedDB). We do not operate external database servers that upload, catalog, or inspect your creative projects.

3. Local Storage & Session Data

The app uses browser storage (localStorage and IndexedDB) strictly for application functionality, such as:

  • Preserving project compositions, layers, and keyframe timelines locally.
  • Remembering your active editor settings and theme preferences.
  • Caching project states for complete offline resilience.

This data remains on your physical device and is never transmitted to any marketing or analytics platforms.

4. Information Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, trade, or otherwise share your personal information, project files, or user data with third parties under any circumstances.

5. Data Deletion and Storage Management

You have full control over your data at all times:

  • Deleting Local Projects: You can delete any project directly inside FishTools Studio using the Project Manager.
  • Clearing Storage: Clearing your browser's site data, cache, or IndexedDB permanently removes all locally stored project files and preferences from your device.
  • Uploaded Share Links: Projects that have been uploaded and converted into a public share link cannot be deleted directly by the user once published. If you need an uploaded project link deleted or removed, please submit a removal request via the Feedback button in the application or contact the developer directly.
